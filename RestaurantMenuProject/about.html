Our Story

We started John's Restaurant in 1988 in Sarasota, Florida and have been aiming to provide quality meals ever since. Our goal here at John's Restaurant is to transform your lunch or dinner into an experience you'll make sure to come back for. All of our food is made fresh and never frozen to meet our quality expectations. We do all of this at an affordable price too so make sure to stop by if you want a delicious and affordable meal!
